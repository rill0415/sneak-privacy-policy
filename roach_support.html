Look Both Ways

Support, FAQ, and privacy for the roach-dodging crossing game on Apple Watch.

Overview Quick Start FAQ Troubleshooting Contact Privacy

Overview

Look Both Ways is a quick, top-down arcade challenge: time your moves, dodge cars, and guide a brave roach to the safety of the far curb. Built for short sessions and snappy haptics.

Quick Start

  • Move: Tap/press to advance one lane when it’s safe.
  • Watch the rhythm: Car patterns repeat—wait for a gap, then dash.
  • Goal: Reach the checkered finish without getting squished.
Tip: If traffic feels desynced, pause a beat—patterns realign quickly.

FAQ

  • Does it require an internet connection? No, it works offline.
  • Accounts or analytics? None. No sign-in, no tracking.
  • Haptics? Subtle taps confirm moves and finishing a level.
  • Accessibility? High-contrast visuals and clear hitboxes.

Troubleshooting

  • No haptics? Check Settings → Sounds & Haptics on Apple Watch.
  • Input delay or stutter? Force-quit and relaunch the app on the watch.
  • Still having issues? Note your watch model and watchOS version, then contact us.

Contact

Email support at youremail@example.com. We try to reply within 3 business days.

Privacy

No personal data is collected. The app does not include analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs. No data leaves your device.
